Diaspora Connect Farmers SACCO

Diaspora Connect is a Farmers SACCO based in Uganda, serving the diaspora community worldwide. Also referred to as Diaspora Connect Farmers SACCO Uganda, DC Farmers SACCO, Diaspora Connect SACCO, and Diaspora Connect Uganda.

Diaspora Connect Farmers SACCO offers diaspora Ugandans the opportunity to invest in productive dairy farmland, participate in collective agricultural savings, and build generational wealth through a trusted cooperative model.
